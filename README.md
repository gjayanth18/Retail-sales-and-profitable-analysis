# 📊 Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes retail sales and profitability to identify key business trends, margin drivers, loss-making product categories, and regional performance.

The project uses **Python and Pandas** for data cleaning and transformation and **Power BI** for interactive business intelligence and dashboard reporting.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Sales revenue alone does not always indicate business success. A product or category may generate high sales while producing low or negative profits.

This project analyzes **8,400+ retail order records** to understand:

* Which product categories generate the highest profit?
* Which sub-categories are negatively impacting profitability?
* How do sales and profit margins vary across regions?
* Are low-profit products affected by pricing or discounting?
* Which areas require business attention to improve profitability?

The analysis transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support pricing and product strategy decisions.

## 📈 Key Business Analysis

### 1. Category-Level Profitability

The analysis compared sales and profit performance across major product categories.

One of the key findings was that **Furniture had a significantly lower profit margin compared with Technology and Office Supplies**.

This highlighted the importance of analyzing **profitability alongside sales**, rather than relying only on revenue figures.

---

### 2. Loss-Making Sub-Categories

Further analysis of the Furniture category identified **Tables and Bookcases** as loss-making sub-categories.

This helped narrow the profitability issue from a broad category-level problem to specific product segments requiring further investigation.

---

### 3. Pricing & Profitability

The analysis suggested that the profitability issue in the identified sub-categories required a **pricing review**, rather than simply attributing the problem to discounting.

This demonstrates how data analysis can move beyond reporting historical performance and help identify potential business actions.

---

### 4. Regional Performance

Sales and profitability were also analyzed across different regions to identify variations in business performance.

The results were incorporated into the Power BI dashboard to allow stakeholders to explore regional trends interactively.
